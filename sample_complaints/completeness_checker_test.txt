We are raising a formal quality complaint regarding a batch of Amoxicillin Trihydrate

Complaint Source:     Direct Customer Complaint (email)
Complaint Date:       19 July 2025

─── NATURE OF COMPLAINT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Complaint Type / Category: Foreign Particulate Matter / Physical Contamination

During our routine incoming quality check (IQC), our QA inspector identified visible
black and dark-brown particulate matter embedded inside the capsule shells across
multiple units in Batch AX-2025-3381. The particles range from 0.5mm to 2mm in size
and appear to be of metallic or carbonaceous origin.

Affected units were quarantined immediately. An estimated 40–50 capsules from two
separate cartons (Carton IDs: C-04 and C-07) were confirmed to contain the particles.
The contamination does not appear to be a surface defect; the particles are encapsulated
within the fill powder, suggesting introduction at the blending or encapsulation stage.

─── SEVERITY & RISK ASSESSMENT ─────────────────────────────────────────────

Severity:   Critical
Priority:   High

This constitutes a potential patient safety risk. Ingestion of foreign particulate matter
of unknown origin — particularly if metallic — could cause harm to patients. We are
placing a full distribution hold on all remaining stock from this batch pending your
investigation and a formal CAPA response.

We request:
  1. Immediate acknowledgement of this complaint within 24 hours
  2. A root cause analysis (RCA) and corrective action plan (CAPA) within 30 days
  3. Written confirmation of your market withdrawal or recall decision, if applicable
