Subject: Complaint - Packaging Defect - Atorvastatin 20mg - Batch AT-2025-0341

Dear Quality Affairs Team,

This is to bring to your attention a packaging-related complaint identified during
routine stock verification at our central warehouse.

Customer Name: Wellness Pharmacy Chain
Product Name: Atorvastatin Calcium Tablets 20mg
Product Strength: 20mg
Batch/Lot Number: AT-2025-0341
Manufacturing Date: January 2025
Expiry Date: December 2026
Quantity Affected: 850 strips (approximately 8500 tablets)

Complaint Type: Packaging Defect

Description of Issue:
Multiple blister strips from this batch were found with improperly sealed cavities,
allowing tablets to move freely and, in a few cases, fall out during transit. Out of
a sample check of 50 strips, 6 strips exhibited this sealing defect. No visible damage
to the tablets themselves was observed, and there is no indication of moisture ingress
or tablet degradation at this time.

While this does not appear to pose an immediate patient safety risk, it raises concerns
about packaging line quality control and could affect product shelf life if moisture
ingress occurs later in distribution.

Severity Assessment: Major
Priority: Medium

We request an investigation into the blister packaging line and corrective action
to prevent recurrence in future batches.

Regards,
Anil Kapoor
Procurement & Quality Coordinator, Wellness Pharmacy Chain
